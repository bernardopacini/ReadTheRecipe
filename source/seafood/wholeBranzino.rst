Whole Branzino
==============

Details
-------

* Number of servings: 4 (1 per 1lb fish)
* Cook time: 25 minutes

Ingredients
-----------

* 4 branzini
* 1 stick (4 ounces) butter
* 1 tablespoon lemon juice
* 1 tablespoon chopped capers
* 1 tablespoon chopped parsely
* 1 lemon
* 4 rosemary sprigs

Steps
-----

#. Preheat oven to 425.
#. Melt the butter in a small bowl.
#. Mix the butter, lemon juice, capers, and parsely, and season with salt.
#. Lay the branzini on their sides in a baking dish.
#. Fill each branzino with sauce, two lemon rounds, and a rosemary sprig.
#. Lightly season the outside of the branzini with leftover sauce and salt.
#. Place in oven, bake for 10-15 minutes (or until each fish begins to flake).

Notes
-----

Unless you are comfortable cleaning and descaling the fish, ask to have the fish prepared when purchasing it.
